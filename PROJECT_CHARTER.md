# Project Charter

Status: Draft
Version: 0.2

## Project
Project Nutrition

## Mission
Build the most practical nutrition and activity ecosystem for individuals, couples and families.

## Vision
Create a platform that combines meal planning, recipes, tracking, shopping, training and activity planning into one coherent experience.

## Target Users
- Individuals
- Couples
- Families
- Users with personal workout plans
- Users following structured short-term nutrition programs

## Product Philosophy
- Real-life usability over theoretical perfection.
- Shared meals with individual nutrition goals.
- Nutrition and activity belong in one daily plan.
- Offline-first.
- AI is optional and never required.
- Simplicity over feature overload.
- Safety rules are deterministic and cannot be overridden by AI.

## Engineering Philosophy
- Modular architecture.
- Clean separation between UI, business logic and backend.
- Deterministic calculations.
- Testable components.
- Cross-platform from day one.

## Success Criteria
The first success is not revenue.

The first success is that Michael and his girlfriend prefer using the app every day over existing alternatives for:

- planning meals
- planning shared and personal activities
- tracking weight and nutrition
- managing shopping
- following structured short-term programs


## Wearable Principle

Wearables are optional convenience integrations.

The app must remain fully useful with manual data entry and without any connected device.


## Human Expert Principle

Project Nutrition may enhance collaboration with real nutrition professionals and personal trainers.

The platform should support human expertise rather than attempting to replace it.

Users control which data is shared, with whom, for how long and at what permission level.
